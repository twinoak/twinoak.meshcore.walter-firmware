## What upstream changed that this session had to answer

Thirteen of upstream's tasks and four releases (`v0.7.0`…`v0.7.3`); their changelog at `b6d4cfb2`
answers its four questions with *no interface moved, no `MeshReply` constant moved, the fixtures'
manifest moved twice, and nothing of yours stops compiling*. That was true — `components/agent/`
compiled untouched — and four things still needed answering.

1. **The loop suite's fake grew four knobs, and our shim of it had not** (the compile errors CI
   stopped at). `host_test/agent-uplink/shim/support/FakeUplink.h` shadows upstream's fake so the
   loop suite can be compiled a second time through `ModemUplink`, and its header promises *every
   knob and every record of the original*. The four are `askForAnUpdate` and `updateResults`
   (p1.15-61), `loseTheAnswerToObservations` and `seenBatches` (p1.13-55), plus
   `Fault::NotFound`; each is copied with the original's meaning and said so beside it. The
   observations route now de-duplicates on the batch's `clientId` in the scripted server, where
   every other route's answer is made, and an answer *lost on the way back* is the TLS refusing
   after the batch has been written into the server's own memory.
2. **One call of `mm::IUplink` has no road here**: `postUpdateResult`. **An update is never asked
   of a Walter** — meshmaster.dk asks it of an exit node running the exit-node firmware's own
   product — so nothing of this repository hands the loop an `mm::IUpdateProvider`, and
   `agent::ModemUplink` takes the library's defaulted body (which is why nothing stopped
   compiling). *What this firmware does with an `update` in a heartbeat's reply after the bump:*
   **nothing, and it never writes the member either.** `AgentLoop` asks a provider for the member
   (`doHeartbeat`, `updateProvider_ != nullptr`), hands a reply's ask to a provider
   (`onUpdateAsk`), posts a result only for a provider (`doUpdateResult` returns at once without
   one) and holds a claim back only when a provider says it holds the jobs — so the heartbeat this
   unit sends is byte for byte the heartbeat it sent, an ask in a reply is read by nobody, and the
   route is never called. Upstream's own case
   `AHeartbeatWithNoUpdateProviderIsTheHeartbeatItAlwaysWas` is exactly this unit, and it passes
   on both roads. Nothing was built to answer an ask. The suite's own update cases do give the
   loop a provider, so the shim answers that one call itself, with the original's `Call` record,
   rather than putting a request this firmware never makes on the wire.
3. **The kept image grew 40 bytes and our sizing was a sum of our own** — the dangerous one, and
   not a compile error anywhere. `mm::kMaxKeptImageLen` is **19 368** bytes at 0.7.3 (the header,
   the result headers over the buffer, a full ring, and the client id of a batch still waiting,
   p1.13-55); `agent::kLargestKeptImage` in `walter_agent/agent_backend.h` added the first four of
   those terms up by hand and would have gone on asserting 19 328 while a full image had become
   19 368. It now **reads the library's constant**, and the two static asserts stand: the figure is
   19 368 and `kKeptScratchBytes` (19 456) holds it, with 88 bytes over where there were 128. The
   store is the window above the image store in `rakfw` — 1 368 064 bytes — so only the scratch was
   ever in question. A follow-up line in `tasks/phase-17/FOLLOWUPS.md` says what the next task that
   wants those 88 bytes has to know.
4. **A console line now carries a prefix, and two things of ours had to learn it** (p1.10-49).
   Every console line of a job goes out as `xy|<line>` and only a reply carrying that prefix is its
   answer. Stock firmware reflects it in `handleCommand` (`examples/simple_repeater/MyMesh.cpp`
   :1211-1215 of MeshCore at the pin `companion-v1.18.0`, `81dbae59`, read in the clone beside
   this one) — so the **simulated** RAK had to reflect it too, or every console line of a job
   through the local door goes unanswered; and the library's **region loader**, whose lines go out
   bare and unawaited, exposed a real refusal of this firmware's own (item 5).
5. **A line nobody waits for left the radio awaiting, and the next line was `ERR_BUSY`** — found by
   upstream's new `AnApplysLinesCarryThePrefixAndTheRegionLoadersDoNot`, and a bug of ours since
   p4-02. `agent/ConfigApply.h` sends a line the firmware answers with nothing (`awaitReply =
   false`) and never awaits it; §6.2's RAK is awaiting from the moment that packet was queued, and
   answers the next send `ERR_BUSY` — which the library reports as *the packet for ' dk F' could
   not be built* and the apply stops at the second line of the load. `LinkMesh` now gives up such
   an exchange before the next send, which is exactly what §6.1's `JOB_BEGIN` again is for and what
   `cancelWait()` already did for a wait this end had given up on; it is counted in
   `never_awaited` beside `cancelled`. **80 bytes of image, and the only firmware change in this
   pull request.**

What else moved and cost nothing, read and checked rather than assumed: `kMeshReplyDataCap` 184 and
`kMeshPathCap` 64 are unmoved at the same MeshCore pin; `library.json`'s only change is the version
(ArduinoJson is still 7.4.3, so `dependencies.lock` agrees and `deps` is green); the four free

  only cases of either suite that this repository does not run through one.
  `ALateRequestReplyDuringTheReLoginIsDroppedAndTheJobRunsOn` and
  `AConsoleReplyThatCameTooLateIsNotStoredAsTheNextLinesValue` both script a reply that came home
  after its own wait and then assert that **the wait goes on** and finds the answer it was waiting
  for: that is the Heltec, whose library reads its own radio. On this road §6.2's RAK awaits *until
  a matching reply arrives or the time is up* and §6.3 reports that outcome **once**, so an
  exchange that was handed a late reply is over and the reply it was waiting for is lost. The
  readings are the same on both roads (`"tx":"22"`, `"name":"Grenaa Chimney #1"`, outcome `ok`,

## Decisions taken without asking

- **The shim answers `postUpdateResult` itself** rather than adding the route to
  `agent::ModemUplink`. The library defaults that call for a consumer that cannot be updated from
  the server, this firmware is one, and the task says to build nothing that answers an update: a
  route no wake can ever reach would be flash for nothing. The shim keeps the original's `Call`
  record for that one call so the loop's own reading of a failure is the same on both roads, and
  the header says why.
- **The lateness of a reply is modelled in the simulated radio, not in the copied fake.** The
  fake's own list counts *waits* as calls of its own `await()`, and through the RAK one wait of the
  job is a thousand of those (the radio polls the repeater a millisecond at a time), so its count
  is spent inside the request's own wait and the reply is not late at all. `sim::SimRak` now holds
  the answer (`late_replies`, `late_reply_waits`) and §6.2 decides which later exchange may read
  it; the shim's `deliverTheNextReplyLate`/`deliverTheNextLineRepliesLate` arm the radio instead of
  the repeater. The rule, the citations and what is *not* modelled are in the code.
- **`LinkMesh` gives up a wait it never made, rather than retrying a send that was refused.** Both
  would work; this one makes no extra `SEND_*` (so `client_tx` and rail R16 are untouched), uses
  the mechanism §6.1 already blesses and the file already has, and leaves the refusal impossible
  rather than recoverable.
- **The bench items and the release note are written for the three changes a person can see** (a
  configuration read's values under their own keys, a region map loadable again, and the same
  adverts not stored twice). The rest of the bump — the repeater's clock in a result, the
  `setperm 0` restore, the owner info whole — is the library's own and was not re-tested here
  beyond its suites.

## Not verified here (and why)

A session can build and run the host tests; it cannot flash a board, reach a network or talk to
meshmaster.dk. Everything below is tested here against simulators only — the library's simulated
stock repeater behind a simulated RAK, and a scripted server behind the firmware's own HTTP/1.1 —
and the same three items are in `docs/bench.md` §26, in the order the rig is brought up.

- [ ] ★ **Every value of a `config.read` is under its own key**, on a neighbour over the mesh and
      on this unit's own repeater through the local door. This is the one item that needs a real
      repeater: stock firmware reflecting the line's prefix is what the library's rule rests on,
      and if a repeater did not reflect it every console line of a job would read *no reply*.
- [ ] **A region map loaded into a repeater by a `config.apply`**: every line of the loader leaves,
      the result carries the repeater's own `OK - loaded N regions`, and no line is reported as
      *the packet could not be built*. That refusal is what this pull request fixed, and it has
      only ever been seen in the simulator.
- [ ] **The same adverts are not stored twice** on the exit node's page after a night of hourly
      wakes, which is the failure of 16 November 2026 that upstream's p1.13-55 is the fix for. It
      needs the real server, because the de-duplication is the server's.
