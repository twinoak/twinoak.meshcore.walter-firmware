A unit now remembers the last eight calls to the server that did not come back — which call, what
came back and when — across a sleep and a power cut, and each wake in its list says why it sent
nothing and how long a wait it still owed. Its check-in also counts what woke it: the timer, the
radio's line, a false alarm, a wake that sent nothing. Seen in the host tests, not yet on a board.
