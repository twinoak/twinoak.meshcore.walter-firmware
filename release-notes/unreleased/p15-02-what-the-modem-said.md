When a call to the server fails, the unit now writes down what its modem did while it waited — how
long each answer took, what it announced, when the other end closed — and keeps it over a sleep and
a power cut. `agent calls` and the unit's report say it. It cures nothing; it is how the failures
costing most wakes half a minute can be read. Seen in the host tests, not yet on a board.
