## What is new

- The unit no longer asks its modem a second time when a call's answer fails to come back: in a night of trying it never once brought the answer, and the second question can cost the wake half a minute of its own. A failing call costs what it cost before, and the line counting those asks is gone from the console and the report. Seen in the host tests, not yet on a board.
- A wake that is cut short by its five-minute bound, a crash or a watchdog now leaves word of what it was in the middle of - the step, the call that was out or the job that was being run, and for how long - and the next boot prints it in the wake list and the journal. The count of such wakes goes out with every check-in. Seen in the simulated bench, not yet on a board.
- The unit's report now prints a call to the server that worked beside the newest one that failed, so the two can be read side by side: every read says when it was asked as well as when it came back, and a call that was answered says what the answer said of itself. The good one is kept only for the wake that made it. Seen in the host tests, not yet on a board.

**Which repeater firmware this goes with.** A unit is two boxes and one key signs both: this
release runs beside
[`twinoak.meshcore.repeater-firmware` v0.1.2](https://github.com/twinoak/twinoak.meshcore.repeater-firmware/releases/tag/v0.1.2),
key `73efdd383a957596`. Nothing in the box link changed with this release, so a radio at that
release needs nothing done to it.

**Taking it from the release before, over LTE.** *Offer a release* on the unit's page on
meshmaster.dk with the Walter's product, then *Apply the candidate*; the unit fetches it on its
next check-in, writes it into its other slot, switches, and is brought back by the bootloader if
the trial fails. A unit that will not come up at all goes by cable, with the factory image and the
`esptool` line below.
