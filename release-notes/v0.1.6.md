## What is new

- meshmaster.dk can now ask a unit what it says about itself and get the lines the console prints — the last wakes and what each did, the agent's state, the uplink, the journal, the settings, the log — without anybody driving to the mast with a cable. It only reads; it changes nothing on the unit. Seen in the simulator and in the host tests, not yet on a board.
- A unit now remembers the last eight calls to the server that did not come back — which call, what came back and when — across a sleep and a power cut, and each wake in its list says why it sent nothing and how long a wait it still owed. Its check-in also counts what woke it: the timer, the radio's line, a false alarm, a wake that sent nothing. Seen in the host tests, not yet on a board.

**Which repeater firmware this goes with.** A unit is two boxes and one key signs both: this
release runs beside
[`twinoak.meshcore.repeater-firmware` v0.1.2](https://github.com/twinoak/twinoak.meshcore.repeater-firmware/releases/tag/v0.1.2),
key `73efdd383a957596`. Nothing in the box link changed with this release, so a radio at that
release needs nothing done to it.

**Taking it from the release before, over LTE.** *Offer a release* on the unit's page on
meshmaster.dk with the Walter's product, then *Apply the candidate*; the unit fetches it on its
next check-in, writes it into its other slot, switches, and is brought back by the bootloader if
the trial fails. A unit that will not come up at all goes by cable, with the factory image and the
`esptool` line below.
